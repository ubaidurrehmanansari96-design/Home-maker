Frequently Asked Questions

What is the design and construction timeline for a residential project?

Timelines vary depending on project scale. Typical luxury interior projects take 8–12 weeks, while full ground-up villa constructions range from 8 to 14 months, strictly following milestone schedules.

Does MY HOMES MAKER handle turnkey projects end-to-end?

Yes. We provide complete end-to-end turnkey solutions covering architectural layout, structural construction, interior execution, custom furniture production, electrical, plumbing, and final styling decor.

How are costs estimated and managed during execution?

We provide itemized BOQs (Bill of Quantities) with absolute transparency prior to project sign-off. Material samples, hardware specifications, and costs are locked in advance to prevent budget overruns.

Can I request custom 3D visualizations before construction begins?

Absolutely. We generate photorealistic 3D renders and 360-degree virtual walkthroughs so you can experience every corner of your future home before construction commences.
